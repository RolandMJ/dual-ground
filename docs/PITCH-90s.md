**Format:** Screen recording with voiceover

## DELIVERY NOTES

**Pace:** Conversational, not rushed. Pause briefly at screen transitions.

**Energy:** Genuine enthusiasm, not hype. You discovered something and you're sharing it.

**Key moments to nail:**
1. "I'm not even a programmer" — builds credibility with non-technical judges
2. The block unlock animation — visually satisfying, shows interactivity
3. "I went from fearing the terminal to loving it" — the emotional payoff
4. Three outputs shown together — proves this isn't a toy, it produces real value
5. "Built solo, in one hackathon" — judges respect scope vs. execution

**What judges are scoring:**
- *Problem clarity* — you nail this in 10 seconds (learning AI is fragmented)
- *Solution elegance* — split-screen learn+build is immediately understandable
- *Execution quality* — let the polish speak: animations, colors, real content
- *Personal story* — you lived the problem, you're not guessing at it
- *Try-it factor* — end with "try it yourself" so they actually visit the link

**Avoid:**
- Don't say "vibe coding" or "hackathon project" — let it stand on its own
- Don't apologise for being non-technical — it's your superpower here
- Don't explain the tech stack — nobody cares in 90 seconds
- Don't rush the terminal section — it's your unique differentiator
